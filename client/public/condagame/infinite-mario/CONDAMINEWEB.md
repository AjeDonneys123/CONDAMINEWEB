# CondaGame integration

The platform game engine in this folder is adapted from [robertkleffner/mariohtml5](https://github.com/robertkleffner/mariohtml5), released under the Unlicense; see `LICENSE.md`.

The surrounding CondaWeb integration adds gem-marked bonus blocks above the ground, reachable with a jump. Hitting a block pauses the game and displays a four-choice QCM from the selected lesson. Correct answers award gems; three gems are required to finish a level. A wrong answer costs one gem, and the player continues the same level. This upstream repository includes Mario-themed artwork and sounds; the engine's Unlicense does not grant rights to Nintendo characters, names, or other third-party assets.
